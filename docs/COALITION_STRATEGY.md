# SpaceЖ — Public Coalition & Outreach Strategy

**Public research · 16 October 2026 · No outreach or institutional commitment implied**

SpaceЖ was conceived to contribute to the **global defense of Ukraine** through verifiable public-source space intelligence, resilient civilian communications research, and independent open infrastructure. It has no operational satellite constellation, tactical targeting capability or live warning service.

## Decision

**Ukrainian requirements first; open engineering second; funding and public advocates only after evidence.** Avoid name-dropping, invented endorsements, and requests for sensitive operational data.

## Suggested 90-day outreach sequence

**Gate (days 1–3):** verify the live pages `/`, `/plan/`, and `/coalition/`. Prepare a one-page English/Ukrainian technical brief, licensed reproducible observation demo, clear capabilities/limitations, and legal entity identification.

**Wave 1 (days 4–10):** ask the six candidates individually for a 20–25-minute critique or needs conversation, after explicit human approval and destination verification. Follow up once after 7–10 business days if appropriate.

**Wave 2 (days 11–30):** incorporate corrections publicly, seek consenting Ukrainian advisors, and scope a lawful pilot. Do not claim their participation until confirmed.

**Waves 3–4 (after a credible pilot):** evaluate grants, fiscal hosting, governance and later high-visibility/industry conversations. Prominent patrons do not get control in exchange for funding. Do not pursue U.S. defense-industry partners.

## Public claims and safeguards

- “We are researching possible reviewers” is permissible; “X supports SpaceЖ” requires evidence and permission.
- Never publish sensitive volunteer station locations, inferred private connections, personal contact data, non-public operational intelligence, or restricted technical data.
- No automated cold-email blasts or scraping of private contact directories.
- No donation checkout, charitable status, fiscal sponsorship, or payment guarantee is established by the partnership plan.
- Capture actual private outreach in Plexus only after privacy controls and human approval.
