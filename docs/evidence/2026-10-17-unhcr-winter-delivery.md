# UNHCR Ukraine winter aid: 2024–2025 targets and reported delivery

Reviewed 17 October 2026 by the SpaceZh coding agent against official UNHCR PDFs, including rendered figures. This is an AI-assisted documentary review, not human editorial approval or an independent audit of delivery. Publisher document bodies and beneficiary photographs are not republished.

Percentages are RELAY calculations, rounded to two decimal places. Denominators are the original plan's matching units; no actual household reach is inferred from people reached. The cash figure is compared with the approximately 550,000-person target, not the 263,000-household target. Targets do not measure all unmet need, and a gap does not establish that people received no aid from another provider or explain its cause.

## Primary source register and locators

1. **[Ukraine Winter Response Plan 2024–2025, updated September 2024](https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/110943)**. The [portal record](https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/details/110943) gives 3 September 2024 as publication and upload date. PDF p. 1, “Main response areas” 1–3, gives every target above. It requests $140 million for cash, $10 million for alternative energy and $20 million for shelter, housing and essential items: $170 million overall. These are financial requirements, not delivery or expenditure.
2. **[UNHCR Ukraine Delivery Updates, 14 April 2025](https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/115718)**. PDF p. 3, winter-cash paragraph, gives 258,345 people assisted from October 2024 to March 2025. P. 4, seasonal infographic, reports the same cash, insulation and essential-item results as the table, and **110 generators**, against 420 targeted. Its 31 March footnote dates people reached, not generator units; a generator-specific cutoff is not established. The footnote also allows data cleaning and retroactive corrections.
3. **[Post Distribution Monitoring: Winter Response 2024–2025](https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/118022)**, document dated July 2025; [portal publication and upload](https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/details/118022) dated 11 August 2025. PDF p. 1, achievements infographic, repeats the three people results and targets; its footnote covers people reached with partners from September 2024 to March 2025 and permits verification, reconciliation and retroactive corrections. P. 4, “Alternative Energy Support,” reports **186 generators distributed during the winter period**. That box does not give individual delivery dates or explain the increase from April's 110. Both dated figures are retained without inventing a reconciliation. P. 1 describes random sampling of beneficiaries for monitoring; this does not turn the publisher's aggregate results into independent verification.
4. **[Cash Assistance Factsheet, Q2 2025](https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/118031)**. [Portal title](https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/details/118031): “UNHCR Ukraine Cash Assistance Factsheet – June 2025”; publication and upload 12 August 2025. PDF p. 1, “Targets and achievements,” **Winter 2024–25** row, repeats 258,345 individuals against 550,000 targeted and reports **$75.8 million disbursed**. This is separate from MPCA 2025 and winter 2025–26 rows.

The October–March cash period in the April update is narrower than the September–March period attached to the PDM's overall people table. These labels are preserved; neither is silently substituted for the other. Multiple UNHCR publications are chronological checks on one institutional account, not independent corroboration.

## What remains unestablished

The reviewed sources do not establish a delivered total for the 2,400 portable-power-station target, spending against the $10 million alternative-energy requirement, or an explanation for the generator difference. The $75.8 million cash disbursement is not alternative-energy spending. Comparing it with the $140 million cash requirement does not establish funding coverage, total programme expenditure, efficiency or fraud; no such ratio is presented.

UNHCR labels 314,000 as multisectoral services. It must not be presented as a deduplicated count of unique beneficiaries. Assistance categories can overlap; their counts are not added to infer unique people. No household records, recipient identities, facility coordinates or current operational details are published.

This is a terminology clarification, not a finding of incorrect target figures or actual expenditure.
