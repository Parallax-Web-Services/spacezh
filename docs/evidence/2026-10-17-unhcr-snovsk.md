# Snovsk winter cash: a documented local registration failure

Checked **2026-10-17T22:11:06Z**. This adds a specific local cause to the [cash-records note](2026-10-17-unhcr-cash-records.md). It does not reconcile the national target gap or the separate 9,326-person discrepancy.

## Supported finding

In case **620/6119/25**, the courts found that failures in beneficiary-list controls and notification deprived **at least 90 Snovsk residents of the opportunity to receive UAH 21,000 winter assistance**, an indicated UAH 1.89 million. The failures included duplicate/address checks, incomplete transfer from initial lists and failure to notify excluded applicants, preventing repeat applications. The appeal court upheld the disciplinary reprimand on **5 November 2025**. This establishes a local administrative failure; it is not a criminal conviction, a payment reconciliation or a count to subtract from UNHCR's national people total.
