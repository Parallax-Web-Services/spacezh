# Contributing to SpaceЖ

SpaceЖ is a proposed public-infrastructure contribution to the **global defense of Ukraine**, beginning with verifiable satellite intelligence and civilian resilience research. It remains at the **public founding proposal** stage, not an operational defense or alert service. We welcome useful criticism.

## Concrete places to begin

- **Engineering:** review the proposed Atlas ingestion, provenance, uncertainty, and reproducibility model.
- **Radio operators:** propose lawful, opt-in ground-station pilot designs compatible with existing networks where possible.
- **Scientists and educators:** propose reproducible use cases with redistributable data, including uses relevant to Ukraine's civilian resilience.
- **Ukrainian civic and research groups:** review priorities and identify safe public-interest requirements; no endorsement is assumed.
- **Security and privacy experts:** review station opt-in, location masking, signed audit trails, and data-license compliance.
- **Community organizers and institutions:** offer pilot-host capacity, documentation, translation, or governance advice.
- **Legal and regulatory experts:** review spectrum, launch, export, charitable-solicitation, and nonprofit formation requirements.

Open an issue proposing one specific deliverable, expected evidence, and any constraints. Avoid publishing a volunteer station's sensitive location, credentials, operational security information, or unlicensed data.

No ownership, reimbursement, charitable status, or funding commitment is implied by participation. Sponsor and donor arrangements require a reviewed policy before funds are accepted.

## Code contributions

The site's HTML and CSS are public for inspection. Project-wide and individual component licenses are **not yet finalized**; contributors should first discuss substantive reusable code changes through an issue. Future open-source components will use explicit approved licenses and contributor requirements when established.

## Coalition contributions

For a public technical review, use the [SpaceЖ coalition-interest issue template](https://github.com/Parallax-Web-Services/spacezh/issues/new?template=coalition-interest.yml). Please describe one specific, verifiable question or deliverable. GitHub issues are **public**: never post sensitive locations, personal contact information, unpublished defense information, credentials or private introductions.

See the [coalition strategy](docs/COALITION_STRATEGY.md). Being named as a research candidate does not establish a relationship with SpaceЖ. Formal collaboration announcements require affirmative permission and evidence. All actual prospect contact history belongs in a private, access-controlled Plexus workspace.
