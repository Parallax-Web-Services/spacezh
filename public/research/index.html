004PRIORITY / PROOF REQUIREMENTS

What evidence could justify a 60-second detection claim?

Exact reliability and false-alert evidence requirements. A hypothetical joint 95% confidence exercise for ≥99% on-time sensitivity and ≤1 expected false alarm per 30 days would need 368 independent zero-miss trials and 110.67 days of zero-false-alarm exposure—only under strict sampling assumptions.

Result: Statistical requirements, not detection performance. 24 mathematical tests passed; no sensor or warning system demonstrated.

001MODEL / HYPOTHETICAL

What can a low-orbit satellite contact actually carry?

A first-order model of an authorized, cooperative 550 km satellite downlink with radio-link budget, link margin and elevation sensitivity. Part of the SpaceЖ Link (communications) workstream, not the early-warning track; published here because the same standard applies.

Result: 6.49 minutes ideal contact; 8.7 dB link margin; 467 kB raw, about 374 kB useful. Not field-tested.

02 / THE RESEARCH CONSTRAINT

Observation is the bottleneck.

A launch cannot be detected by software if no independent suitable source observed it.

Let C mean a launch was observable by a suitable, independent sensor, and D₆₀ mean it was correctly verified within 60 seconds. Because timely verified detection requires observation:

P(D₆₀) ≤ P(C)

This is a mathematical ceiling, not a measured coverage result. Research 003 examines correlated observations and timestamp uncertainty. Research 004 quantifies the sample sizes and false-alarm monitoring a defensible future claim would require.

03 / INDEPENDENCE REQUIREMENT

No U.S. warning dependency.

The entire future critical path must be independently verifiable.

A European-hosted copy of U.S.-origin military warning data would not satisfy independence. Neither would a system that required U.S. military sensing, approval or indispensable operational infrastructure.

We have not identified a ready-made independent sensor feed that proves 60-second launch coverage. The study makes that uncertainty explicit rather than substituting a simulated alert for a real observation.

Any real civilian warning work must be coordinated with authorized Ukrainian institutions. SpaceЖ does not issue alerts, track missiles or provide tactical targeting data.
