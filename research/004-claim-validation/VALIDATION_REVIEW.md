# Research 004 — External Scientific Review Questions

**Public, non-operational protocol for qualified Ukrainian scientific or civilian-safety review**

1. **Scope and clock:** Is the measured endpoint correctly anchored to actual event time (not a delayed partner notice) and is a range of event timestamps sufficient to reject unresolved cases? Do not request protected event timestamps or sensor sites in public.
2. **Sampling frame:** Could a properly authorized evaluator establish a complete denominator including unobserved events? If not, what *limited* claim could be supported instead of population sensitivity?
3. **Dependence:** In what conditions do launch events cluster or change characteristics enough to invalidate binomial iid assumptions? We seek general methodology, not operational categories, sensor layouts or deployment parameters.
4. **False alarms:** What definition and observation exposure should be pre-registered? How should periods of disabled monitoring, source outages and threshold revisions be counted?
5. **Independence:** Which categories of provenance evidence would demonstrate a critical path without indispensable U.S.-origin operational sensing? No restricted service credentials or classified feeds requested.
6. **Public safety:** What institutional approval would be necessary before any real evaluation? Should all public outputs remain aggregated, delayed and redacted?
7. **Decision:** Which failures should automatically bar a public 60-second detection claim? Which proposed results could be reported solely as progress toward observational feasibility?

Any formal evaluation would require separate institutional authorization, privacy/security review and appropriate legal governance. This proposal does not solicit or process operational missile data or implement detection, tracking, weapon employment, or alerting.
