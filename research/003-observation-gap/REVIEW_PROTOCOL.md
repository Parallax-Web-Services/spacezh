# Research 003 — independent scientific review protocol

**Draft for qualified Ukrainian and non-U.S. scientific/public-safety review; not a request for classified data or operational access.**

## What a valid claim must actually establish

- **Event definition:** What constitutes a verified launch event? Who is permitted to validate that definition? Do not make operational threat information public.
- **Time origin:** The evaluation clock starts at the verified underlying event, not when a vendor's message arrives. Document uncertainty intervals.
- **Independent sensing:** Show a documented and legally accessible observation source genuinely capable of providing launch evidence, not an orbit catalogue or redistributor of another nation's restricted feed.
- **Completeness:** Construct an unbiased denominator with *all* verified events in the permitted sample, including misses and late recognitions. Negative windows must be specified without cherry-picking.
- **Separate milestones:** Record first suitable observation, completed verification, any separately authorized official public alert, and end-device arrival **only as legally permitted aggregate statistics**. Passing one stage does not establish the rest.
- **Joint failures:** Establish which data feeds reflect the same upstream sensor, operator, infrastructure or decision authority. Do not assume independence merely because there are two endpoints or distributors.
- **Reliability and false alarms:** Report confidence/uncertainty and base rates; document what happens when inputs are absent or timestamps disagree. Safety authorities—not SpaceЖ—must set acceptable thresholds.
- **Critical-path sovereignty:** Confirm that indispensable data, permission, timekeeping, computing, transport and operations remain functional without U.S. military sources or indispensable U.S.-controlled services. Unknown provenance means unknown independence.

## No-go conditions

- No suitably early, independently evidenced observations.
- Launch reference time or full independent denominator unavailable.
- Upstream dependence or right to process the data unresolved.
- False-positive exposure and potential civilian harms unreviewed.
- An evaluator is trained or tuned only on already-detected examples.
- The research team cannot distinguish source latency from launch-to-event latency.
- Someone proposes sending public alerts from an experimental system without authority.

## Public release format

A report may publish: reviewed source categories; methodological definitions; redacted provenance graph at an abstract level; grouped positive/negative counts where safely allowed; timing-confidence interval methods; statistical uncertainty; and explicit insufficiencies. It must not publish protected sources, sensor performance specifications, live missile tracks, launch coordinates, positions of sensitive volunteers, secrets or targeting/impact predictions.

## One useful first request

“Would your institution be willing to critique this publicly available **validation methodology**, particularly the distinction between an independently observed event and a redistributed alert? We do not request classified operational data or existing defense sensor access. No partnership or endorsement is implied.”
