# SpaceЖ — Open research

Our mission is to support Ukraine's civilian protection and independence with research that can be checked, challenged, and reproduced. Each study begins with an engineering decision, publishes assumptions, includes negative cases, and distinguishes simulation from measurement.

## Publication standard

- Publish inputs, methods, reproducible code, tests, assumptions, error bounds and known limitations.
- Record genuine observations separately from simulated inputs; a visualization is not independent evidence.
- Do not cherry-pick only positive detections; missing observations and false alarms matter.
- Two distributor feeds of the same upstream observation are not two independent sources; proposed future capabilities are not operational evidence.
- Do not share private sensor details, ground-station coordinates, tactical targeting data or live threat tracks.
- Never issue an unauthorized emergency alert. Future civilian-warning evaluation requires Ukrainian institutional authority and independent safety review.

We will report an unresolved or infeasible result rather than invent an operational capability.
